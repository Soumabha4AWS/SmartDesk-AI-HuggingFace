# Employee Onboarding Knowledge Base

## Overview

This document provides guidelines and procedures for onboarding new employees. The onboarding process ensures that new team members have the required tools, access, knowledge, and support to successfully start their role.

---

# 1. Day-One Checklist

## Purpose

To ensure a smooth and productive first day for new employees.

---

## New Employee Checklist

### Before Joining

- Complete required joining documentation.
- Submit identity and employment verification documents.
- Review company policies and guidelines.
- Confirm joining date and reporting details.

---

## First Day Activities

### HR Orientation

- Welcome session with HR.
- Introduction to company values and culture.
- Completion of employee documentation.
- Overview of benefits and policies.

### Manager Introduction

- Introduction to team members.
- Discussion of role responsibilities.
- Review of initial goals and expectations.
- Explanation of team processes and communication channels.

### System Access

Verify access to:

- Corporate email
- Employee portal
- Collaboration tools
- Source code repositories (if applicable)
- Project management tools
- Required business applications

---

## Employee Confirmation

New employees should confirm:

- Required access received.
- Equipment received.
- Policies reviewed.
- Questions or concerns raised with HR/manager.

---

# 2. IT Equipment Allocation

## Purpose

To provide employees with required hardware and software resources.

---

## Standard Equipment

Depending on role requirements, employees may receive:

- Laptop/Desktop
- Monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset
- Security accessories
- Other approved peripherals

---

## Allocation Process

1. HR shares new employee details with IT.
2. IT prepares required equipment.
3. Security policies are applied.
4. Employee receives equipment on joining day.
5. Employee signs equipment acknowledgement form.
6. IT confirms successful setup.

---

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

- Protect company-issued equipment.
- Use devices only for authorized business purposes.
- Report lost or damaged equipment immediately.
- Follow security guidelines.

---

## Equipment Return Process

Employees must return all company assets during:

- Resignation
- Role transition
- Contract completion

IT verifies returned equipment before clearance.

---

# 3. Benefits Enrollment

## Purpose

To help employees enroll in company-provided benefits.

---

## Available Benefits

Benefits may include:

- Health insurance
- Life insurance
- Retirement plans
- Paid leave benefits
- Wellness programs
- Employee assistance programs

---

## Enrollment Process

1. HR provides benefits information.
2. Employee reviews available options.
3. Employee completes enrollment forms.
4. HR validates submitted details.
5. Benefits become active based on company rules.

---

## Required Information

Employees may need to provide:

- Personal details
- Dependent information
- Bank details
- Nominee information
- Required supporting documents

---

## Changes to Benefits

Employees should update HR for:

- Marriage
- Addition of dependents
- Change in personal information
- Life events affecting benefits

---

# 4. Buddy Programme

## Purpose

To help new employees adapt quickly to the organization, team culture, and workplace processes.

---

## Buddy Responsibilities

A buddy helps the new employee with:

- Understanding team structure
- Navigating company tools
- Learning workplace practices
- Answering general questions
- Understanding communication channels

---

## Buddy Programme Process

1. HR/manager assigns a buddy.
2. Buddy connects with the new employee.
3. Regular interactions are scheduled during onboarding.
4. Buddy provides guidance during the initial transition period.

---

## Recommended Activities

Buddy may help with:

- Office/team introduction
- Project walkthrough
- Development environment setup
- Company culture understanding
- Finding internal resources

---

# 5. Probation Period

## Purpose

To evaluate the employee's suitability, performance, and alignment with company expectations.

---

## Duration

The probation period is typically defined in the employment agreement.

---

## During Probation

Employees are expected to:

- Understand role responsibilities.
- Complete assigned tasks.
- Demonstrate required skills.
- Follow company policies.
- Build effective working relationships.

---

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers should:

- Provide regular feedback.
- Define clear expectations.
- Support employee development.
- Review progress periodically.

---

## Probation Review Process

### Performance Evaluation

Assessment may include:

- Quality of work
- Productivity
- Technical capability
- Team collaboration
- Communication skills
- Adherence to policies

---

### Possible Outcomes

At the end of probation:

- Probation successfully completed.
- Probation extended with additional goals.
- Employment decision based on performance review.
