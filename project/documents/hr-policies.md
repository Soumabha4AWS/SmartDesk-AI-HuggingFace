# HR Policies Knowledge Base

## Overview

This document provides guidelines for common employee HR-related processes and policies. Employees should refer to this document for information regarding leave, remote work, reimbursements, workplace behavior, and performance management.

---

# 1. Leave Policy

## Purpose

To define the process and guidelines for requesting and managing employee leave.

## Types of Leave

### Annual Leave Entitlements

Annual Leave Entitlements: CL: 6 days/year; SL: 8 days/year; EL: 18 days/year; Carry-forward up to 30; Encashment per policy.

### Casual Leave (CL)

**Purpose:**
Used for personal work, short-term requirements, or unforeseen situations.

**Guidelines:**

- Employees should apply for casual leave in advance whenever possible.
- Approval from the reporting manager is required.
- Unused casual leave may expire at the end of the leave year depending on company policy.
- Casual leave cannot normally be combined with extended leave unless approved.

---

### Sick Leave (SL)

**Purpose:**
Used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or medical reasons.

**Guidelines:**

- Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible.
- Medical documentation may be required for extended sick leave.
- Sick leave approval follows company HR guidelines.
- Employees should update their expected return date when recovering.

---

### Earned Leave (EL) / Annual Leave

**Purpose:**
Provided to employees for planned personal time off.

**Guidelines:**

- Employees should submit leave requests in advance.
- Manager approval is required before taking leave.
- Leave balance can be checked through the HR portal.
- Carry-forward and encashment rules depend on company policy.

---

## Leave Request Process

1. Log in to the HR portal.
2. Select **Leave Management**.
3. Choose leave type.
4. Enter leave dates and reason.
5. Submit request.
6. Manager reviews and approves/rejects the request.
7. Employee receives notification.

# 2. Work From Home (WFH) Guidelines

## Purpose

To provide flexibility while maintaining productivity and business continuity.

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely based on:

- Role requirements
- Manager approval
- Business needs
- Company remote work policy

---

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees working from home must:

- Maintain regular working hours.
- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Be available through approved communication tools.
- Ensure secure handling of company information.
- Maintain a professional work environment.

---

## Security Guidelines

Employees must:

- Use company-approved devices.
- Connect through VPN when required.
- Avoid accessing confidential information from public networks.
- Follow data protection policies.

---

## WFH Approval Process

1. Submit WFH request through HR portal.
2. Provide reason and duration.
3. Obtain manager approval.
4. HR records the approved arrangement.

---

# 3. Reimbursement Process

## Purpose

To reimburse employees for approved business-related expenses.

## Eligible Expenses

Examples:

- Business travel expenses
- Client meeting expenses
- Internet expenses (if applicable)
- Approved software/tools
- Office-related expenses

---

## Submission Process

1. Collect valid receipts and invoices.
2. Log in to the expense management system.
3. Enter expense details.
4. Upload supporting documents.
5. Submit for manager approval.
6. Finance team reviews and processes payment.

---

## Requirements

Employees must provide:

- Expense date
- Business purpose
- Amount
- Receipt/invoice
- Approval details (if required)

# 4. Code of Conduct

## Purpose

To maintain a professional, respectful, and ethical workplace.

## Employee Expectations

Employees must:

- Treat colleagues with respect.
- Maintain professional behavior.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Follow company policies and procedures.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Use company resources responsibly.

---

## Prohibited Activities

Employees must not:

- Engage in discrimination or harassment.
- Misuse company assets.
- Share confidential information without authorization.
- Participate in unethical or illegal activities.

---

## Reporting Violations

Employees can report concerns through:

- HR department
- Manager
- Ethics hotline
- Confidential reporting channels

---

# 5. Anti-Harassment Policy

## Purpose

To provide a safe workplace free from harassment and inappropriate behavior.

## Policy Statement

The company maintains zero tolerance toward harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation.

---

## Examples of Prohibited Behavior

- Unwanted verbal comments
- Offensive jokes or messages
- Discriminatory behavior
- Workplace intimidation
- Unwelcome physical contact
- Sexual harassment

---

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees should:

- Maintain respectful workplace interactions.
- Report inappropriate behavior.
- Cooperate with investigations.
- Maintain confidentiality during investigations.

---

## Reporting Process

1. Report the concern to:
   - HR department
   - Manager
   - Internal complaint committee

2. HR reviews the complaint.
3. Investigation is conducted.
4. Appropriate action is taken based on findings.

---

# 6. Performance Review Cycle

## Purpose

To evaluate employee performance, provide feedback, and support career growth.

---

## Review Frequency

Typical performance review cycle:

- Annual performance review
- Mid-year feedback discussion
- Continuous feedback sessions

---

## Performance Evaluation Areas

Employees are evaluated on:

- Achievement of goals
- Technical skills
- Quality of work
- Collaboration
- Innovation
- Leadership capabilities
- Professional development

---

## Performance Review Process

### Step 1: Goal Setting

- Employee and manager define yearly objectives.
- Goals are aligned with business priorities.

### Step 2: Continuous Feedback

- Regular discussions between employee and manager.
- Progress tracking throughout the year.

### Step 3: Self Assessment

Employee provides:

- Achievements
- Challenges
- Learning activities
- Future goals

### Step 4: Manager Assessment

Manager evaluates:

- Performance results
- Skill development
- Business impact

### Step 5: Final Review

- Review discussion takes place.
- Feedback is documented.
- Development plans are created.
